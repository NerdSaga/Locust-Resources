Accessibility statement

We want the Sacramento Volunteer Help Center to be usable by as many people as possible.

Our accessibility goal

We are working toward meeting the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities.

We are continually working to make this help center more accessible. As the website grows, we will review its content and features, address barriers we discover, and use feedback from volunteers to guide further improvements.

Accessibility features

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, search, and FAQ accordions
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators
  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and link text
  • A skip link for reaching the main content
  • Responsive layouts that support mobile devices and text enlargement
  • Reduced animation for people who prefer reduced motion

Need help or found a barrier?

If something on this website is difficult to access, please tell us what page or feature you were using, what happened, and what device or assistive technology you were using. We will use that information to improve the experience.
